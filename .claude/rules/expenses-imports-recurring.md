# Expenses, imports and recurring expenses

## Expenses, imports and recurring expenses

- Expenses (`Expense`, `ExpenseService`) are non-fuel costs of a vehicle (free-text category, optional odometer reading; the cost's optional `Discount` since #204: `Amount` is what was paid, `discount` on `addExpense`/`updateExpense`/`markRecurringExpensesDone`, left out (or null) on an update keeps the expense's and 0 clears it (`KEEP_WHEN_NULL.expenses`; a build from before discounts sends none), the dialogs send none while the amount is empty (the Done dialog says at the field that a discount needs the amount, since the amount is optional there), and a photo's discount follows its total (`{ discount: 'amount' }`), refused without an amount (`ExpenseService.Build`, `MarkDoneAsync` before anything moves), `Expense.Create` re-normalises the cost with it; the Expenses grid's `discount` column is `defaultHidden`, its query asks for `currency` with `$withDiscount` too; an expense receipt's `discount` is read without a cross-check and filled only with the amount the photo creates); they use the vehicle's *logs* access rules and trash like refuelings. One a tyre log line's amount made (#288, `TyreEntry.ExpenseId`, no foreign key) shares the line's day and its reading row, so `ExpenseService.UpdateAsync`/`DeleteAsync`/`RestoreAsync` hand it to `TyreService` (`LineOfExpenseAsync`; no circle: `TyreService` uses the expense repository only), `ExpenseRepository.PurgeAsync` keeps a reading a line holds and clears the line's link, and GraphQL's `Expense.tyreEntry` names the line (`.claude/rules/tyres.md`). Lines made together for several tyres (#289) share one expense: every live line holding it follows its date and odometer, a photo's reading reaches each of them, `ExpenseTyreEntryLoader` lists them all (`Expense.tyreEntries`, live ones first, each with its tyre's label and group) and `tyreEntry` stays the first live one, which the chip and the banner use (*Open in the tyre log* opens the row it is in). An expense's tyre details (#290, `tyre` on `addExpense`/`updateExpense`, `ExpenseTyreDetails`) put it on the tyre log, or change what its lines say: `ExpenseService` hands them to `TyreService` (`AttachLinesAsync` for an add or a plain expense's edit, `UpdateExpenseAsync` for one the tyre log made), so the expense and its lines are saved in one (`.claude/rules/tyres.md`). A visit takes them too (`markRecurringExpensesDone(tyre)`, `MarkDoneInput.Tyre`, the owner's decisions 57 and 63): with an amount (or photos) they go with its `ExpenseService.AddAsync`, without one `TyreService.AddEntryAsync` logs the line(s) alone on the visit's day with its odometer, a reading checked before any schedule moves (`RecurringExpenseService` → `TyreService`, no circle); the lines' ids are named first (the client's, else new), so a failure of the schedules' save takes them back with the expense (`TyreService.TakeBackAsync`, to the trash) and a try again brings them back (`RestoreTakenBackAsync`), unless a twin saved the schedules at this visit meanwhile (a newer saved version at its date and odometer). A visit without an expense has nothing linking it (#330, the owner's option A): sent again (a lost answer pushed later under a change id the ledger never saw), it is answered as recorded, nothing restored or moved, when every line it names by the client's ids is there (`TyreService.LineStatesAsync`: this vehicle, this person, live or in the trash) and every schedule stands at its day (and odometer, when given); read before anything is made, so a failure takes back only the lines this request made or brought back, never one live before it. Its known limits: a plain visit (no lines) sent twice moves the version once; a failed first try followed by another visit on the same day reads as recorded, its lines staying in the trash, restorable; a recorded visit sent after a later one is parked with `recurring.doneBeforeLast`; lines whose ids the server chose are never recognised; a line a failed try could not take back (its take-back failed too, only a warning) is live before the next try, so a second failure leaves it on the tyre log. The Application tests' recurring fake puts a failed save's schedules back as last saved. The Done dialog's section is the Expense dialog's (`useExpenseTyreDetails`, `TyreDetailsFields`), the schedules' title staying (decision 64), the note and the toast saying a line without an amount is logged alone. Importing (`Application/Imports`): one `IImportParser` per format turns a file into an `ImportBatch` (`FuelioCsvParser` first); `ImportService` keeps the batch under a token in `ImportSessionStore` (in memory, 30 minutes, per user), previews duplicates and commits row by row through `RefuelingService`/`ExpenseService`/`RecurringExpenseService` (schedules last, so a distance schedule without a known odometer starts from the imported readings), so all rules apply and bad rows are reported, not fatal; repeating Fuelio reminder templates become `ImportedRecurring` (duplicates: same title). A new format = a new parser + its tests; the UI (`pages/ImportPage.tsx`) lists formats in `FORMATS`.
- Recurring expenses (`Domain/Recurring`, `Application/Recurring`): `RecurringExpense` is a schedule of a vehicle (kind Time, Odometer or Combined = whichever is reached first; interval in months and/or distance; baseline = last done date and odometer; per-item warn days/distance). It is not a cost (no `Cost`) and its baseline odometer is a plain number, not an `OdometerReading`, so it never takes part in neighbour validation. `RecurrenceCalculator` (pure) turns it plus today and the vehicle's latest reading into a `RecurrenceStatus` (Upcoming/DueSoon/Overdue, which limit decides, days and distance left); the service computes it on every read, nothing is stored (`Vehicle.recurring` goes through `RecurringByVehicleLoader` -> `ListForVehiclesAsync`, so a list of vehicle cards costs a fixed number of queries, not several per card). `RecurringExpenseService` uses the vehicle's *logs* access rules through `LogAccessGuard` (like expenses and photos); `MarkDoneAsync(ids, input)` marks a service visit: schedules of one vehicle (another vehicle's or an unknown id is `recurring.notFound`; ids deduped; none is `recurring.noneSelected`), one Edit check, `CheckDone` on every one first (its errors carry the schedule's `title`: `doneBeforeLast`, `doneOdometerRequired`, `odometerBelowLast`; `Apply` keeps `recurring.odometerRequired`), then, when an amount or photos came, ONE `Expense` through `ExpenseService` (all expense/odometer/photo rules; title/category default to `RecurringDoneDefaults`: titles joined and cut at 120, common or first category, the note only for a single schedule), then `CompleteAsync` saves the moved baselines and the `RecurringCompletion` links (expense id + schedule id) in one save; a failure trashes the expense (`TryTrashAsync`). All or nothing; the amount is never split. `RecurringCompletions` has one foreign key, cascading from the schedule; the expense side has none (expenses and schedules both cascade from the vehicle, and SQL Server refuses two cascade paths), so `ExpenseRepository.PurgeAsync` removes the links in code like photo rows. `Expense.schedules { id title }` comes through `ExpenseSchedulesLoader` (the Expenses grid's `schedules` column, `defaultHidden`). Schedules are hard-deleted (no trash); the table cascades with the vehicle and `UserDataRepository` moves or purges them. GraphQL: `Vehicle.recurring`, `add/update/deleteRecurringExpense`, `markRecurringExpensesDone` (`{ schedules, expense }`), `Expense.schedules`. UI: the vehicle page's Recurring tab (`RecurringPanel` with a selection column and *Mark selected as done*, `RecurringFormDialog`, `RecurringDoneDialog`: the vehicle's schedules as labelled checkboxes, `recurringDone.ts` `preselect` ticks the clicked one plus every other one not `UPCOMING` of its category (#161, `sameCategory`: both non-empty once trimmed, compared by one `Intl.Collator` of the runtime's default locale with `sensitivity: 'accent'`, made on first use and kept (`localeCompare` with options would build one per call, for every row on every render), so case does not count and accents do; one without a category starts alone; the `DoneItem` type requires `category`, so a list without it does not compile), optional amount, title/category prefilled from the ticked ones until typed) and, on the home cards, attention badges (`useDueText`, `RecurringStatusBadge`) each with a Done button that opens `RecurringDoneDialog` right there (see Home cards).
- **Importing cars from a Tankstat backup or export** (#336, the first step; #337 builds the rest; the reader itself is in `backups.md`): `POST /imports/tankstat` (`ImportEndpoints`, a route of its own beside `/imports/{format}`) streams the file to the session's scratch folder. `BackupImportService.RequireUploaderAsync` runs **before the body is read**: signed in, the switch (`Imports:BackupEnabled`; off, only real administrators, and with sign-in off nobody), the announced length against `Imports:MaxUploadMb` (200); Kestrel's own limit is lifted only then and every byte is counted. GraphQL: `backupImport(token)` (the status, asked again while `OPENING`), `openBackupImport` (a passphrase), `endBackupImport`, `canImportFromBackup`.
  - **The session** (`IBackupImportSessions`, `Infrastructure/Backups/Reader/BackupImportSessions`): **one per person** (a new upload ends the earlier one and deletes its folder at once), 30 minutes from its last use, swept by a timer every minute, on the server that took the upload and in its memory (another server behind the same address does not see it: a named limit); the scratch folder is `Imports:ScratchFolder` (the system's temporary folder unless set, an environment-level setting like every folder) + `tankstat-import`, **emptied whole at start**, so every test host gets its own (`TestApp.ImportScratchPath`). A deleted account's sessions end (`UserService`). `Hold` keeps a session from being replaced (`import.fileInUse`, said before the upload's body is taken and looked at again when the file replaces the earlier one) or expiring while a run reads it (#337, #340); a hold, a replacing upload's and the sweep's look at the holds go under one lock, so a hold never lands between that look and the session's end; `End` (the person giving the file up) does not look at holds: #337's to decide. **A session ended while its file is being opened** (a new upload, the step left, the account gone, a stop): its folder is deleted at once, and again by the open itself once it has wound down (`OpenAsync`'s `finally`: the first delete runs under the open's writes and can miss a file, or meet one made after it); `StopAsync` waits for the opens under way (never throws), as `BackupRunner`'s does, so a restart in the process never empties the scratch folder under one; tests that look at the folder after ending such a session wait with `WhenIdleAsync()`. The cars' database is deleted without throwing (`Discard`: the sweep runs on a timer), the folder's delete reporting what could not go. An ended session says why for five minutes, then is forgotten (`import.expired`).
  - **Keys and tries:** an upload takes its **passphrase only**, three tries, then the file is removed (`import.noTriesLeft`); a restore point (`OpenFileAsync`, for #340) takes a passphrase or an identity, without a limit, keeps the users' e-mails, and is never copied or deleted. One try at a time per session (a second submit while one runs is that try), one Argon2 derivation at a time per server. The passphrase and the identity are locals of the try: nothing keeps them.
  - **Reading and comparing:** `IBackupCars.ReadCarAsync` gives a car's refuellings, expenses (with the schedules its visit marked done) and schedules as the services' inputs with their ids (`BackupCarShapes`), what was in the trash left out. `BackupCarComparer.Compare(backup, target)` (pure): by **id inside the target car** first, then by key (a refuelling by date and odometer, an expense by date, lowercased title and amount, a schedule by lowercased title); several of the backup on one of the target's: the first by date then id takes it. *Differs* lists the input's fields with both sides' values (an empty text is none, 40.0 is 40); never what is worked out. Entries come in pages (`Page`, at most 200). The target's trash is #337's to look at.
  - **The step** (`pages/import/BackupFileStep.tsx`, not offered on the page until #337 adds the source to `FORMATS`): `uploadBackupImport` (XHR, for the upload's share), then the status asked every second while `OPENING`; the passphrase at a `Field` that says a wrong one with the tries left and takes the focus; leaving the step with a file still on the server ends it there. Texts `import.backup.*` and `errors.import.*`.
