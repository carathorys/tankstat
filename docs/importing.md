# Importing

For people who bring their logs from another app.

The *Import* page walks through four steps: choose the file, choose the target (an existing vehicle you may add logs to, or a new one), review, done. Nothing is saved before you confirm.

- The file is uploaded raw with `POST /imports/{format}` (the answer is a token for the parsed file, kept in memory for 30 minutes); the preview (`importPreview`) and the confirmation (`confirmImport`) are GraphQL.
- **Fuelio** (`fuelio`): the "sync" CSV export of one vehicle (unzip it first). Fuel logs, other costs (with their category names) and the vehicle's name, plate, units and fuel type are read; reminder templates that repeat (every so many months and/or kilometres) become recurring expenses, counting from where the reminder stands now (their amount is not kept; one-off reminders are skipped), a log Fuelio marks as following a missed fill-up keeps that mark, stations, GPS and weather are ignored, income rows are skipped, and a cost with odometer 0 gets no odometer. Fuelio files carry no currency, so you enter one (it defaults to `Defaults:Currency`). Dates keep only the day.
- Rows are saved through the same services as rows typed by hand, so every rule applies (access, odometer order against the vehicle's other readings, future dates). A row that breaks a rule is reported and the rest is still imported. For an existing vehicle you choose whether rows that already exist (same date and odometer; same date, title and amount for expenses) are skipped or imported again.
- More formats (other CSV or JSON sources) are one more `IImportParser` that turns a file into an `ImportBatch` (see [Import formats](development.md#import-formats)).
- **A Tankstat backup or export**: cars out of a backup file of this app, or of an export made on its Import and export page (`.zip`). See [Cars from a backup](#cars-from-a-backup).

## Cars from a backup

Choose *Tankstat backup or export* as the source on the Import page (with `Imports:BackupEnabled` turned off it is offered to administrators only, and with sign-in off to nobody).

1. **File.** Upload the backup (up to `Imports:MaxUploadMb`, 200 MB by default). An encrypted one asks for its passphrase: three tries, then the file is removed from the server; the passphrase is used for that try and kept nowhere. The file waits on the server for 30 minutes after its last use, one file per person, and for as long as an import of it waits or runs. A file is refused as not being a backup when one of its cars holds more than 50,000 refuellings, expenses or recurring expenses, or a text longer than the app itself accepts.
2. **Cars.** The file's cars, each with whose it was and how much it holds. Tick the ones you want. A car comes **as a new car**, or is **added to one of your own cars** (the app suggests one when the name or the license plate matches). A car can only be added to a car in the same units; numbers are never converted. Cars that were in the backup's trash are left out.

   Under a ticked car, **Choose what to import** says what comes of its refuellings, expenses and recurring expenses. For a new car each kind comes whole, or not at all. For a car added to one of yours, each kind says how many of the backup's entries are only there, differ from yours, or are the same, and offers:
   - *Don't import*: nothing of that kind.
   - *Only the missing* (the default): the entries only in the backup are added; the ones that differ stay as they are.
   - *Replace all*: your car's entries of that kind, those in its trash too, are **deleted for good**, then all of the backup's come in. Their photos go with them, for good too; the backup's photos of its entries come back when *Photos of entries* is ticked. It is not offered while one of your expenses belongs to a tyre log line (its amount could not come back to a set that stays), or when the backup has none of that kind.
   - *Choose…*: a dialog lists the entries only in the backup (tick the ones you want) and the ones that differ (with what differs; keep yours or take the backup's values), 50 at a time.

   **Tyres** come set by set, under the same button: a box per tyre set of the backup, with its season and how many times on the car, tyre log lines and single tyres it has. New sets start ticked. A set that is already in your car (the same name, season, size and *in use since* month) stays as it is and is shown without a box to tick; a set your car's history cannot take (another set was on the car then, or its tyres' labels are taken) is shown with why and cannot be ticked. A set comes **with its history**: its times on the car, its tyre log lines, its single tyres with their corners and replacements, its price, and the expenses its lines hold (those come with the tyres, not with the expenses, so a cost is never there twice). *Lines not tied to a set* come as one row (in a car of yours, only the missing ones). The swap reminders of imported sets start afresh. An amount already among your car's expenses (an earlier import without the tyres brought it) stays there: a line that can hold it is tied to it, a swap's fee, a set's price or a replacement's price comes without it, and the report says so.

   **The rest of the car**, under the same button: *Photos of entries* (a photo comes when its entry is imported or already in your car, and not when the same file is among that entry's photos already; none when the backup was made without the photos), *The car's picture* (not ticked when your car has one: ticked, the backup's replaces it for good), *Charts* (the shared ones, and your own ones from your own backup or export; one with the same title and recipe here is not made again; charts that were someone else's own are not imported) and *The dashboard* (not ticked when your car has its own: ticked, the backup's replaces it for everyone who sees the car) and *My own dashboard*, a box of its own for your personal dashboard of the car from your own backup or export, not ticked when you have one here). A tile of the dashboard whose chart did not come is left out, and the report says how many.

   A car with every kind left out, and no tyres, cannot go on: leave the car out instead.
3. **Review.** Per car: what is new, what already exists and is skipped, what differs from yours and stays as it is, and what is in your car's trash (not imported; restore it there if you want it). For each car you choose what happens when an entry cannot be saved: *Import the rest* (the default; the entry is listed in the report) or *Leave this car out* (nothing of that car comes in; the other cars still do). A car with a *Replace all* is **all or nothing**: if one of its entries cannot be saved, or one of its entries got a photo or a tyre log line since the review, it stays exactly as it was. A visit (an expense that marked recurring expenses done) keeps its link when neither of the two kinds is left out.

   **Import** then asks, for each car with a *Replace all*, one after the other, that you type the car's name: what is deleted is listed, with how many of the visits' links will be lost and how many of its photos the backup does not bring back. *Cancel* on any of them starts nothing. Entries you change or add after the review are deleted with the rest, and *Take the backup's* overwrites an entry changed since the review.
4. **Done.** The report: per car what came in, what was skipped, and each entry that could not be saved with its reason; per kind with a choice of its own, what was replaced (deleted and imported), imported and changed. A car put back is headed "left as it was", with why.

What you import **becomes yours**. Sharing, who logged an entry and marks for review are not carried over. Refuellings, expenses, recurring expenses, tyres, the photos of entries, the car's picture, its charts and its dashboard come in; reading a photo is not started for them, and their entries are not marked for review.

An import is **all or nothing**: when it is cancelled, takes longer than the server allows (`Runs:LimitMinutes`), or the server restarts while it runs, nothing of it is kept and your cars are as they were. While it runs, saving may have to wait for a moment, for you and for others. One import runs at a time on an instance: another waits in line and starts by itself (for up to 30 minutes). You can leave the page; a notification tells you when it is done and leads to the report, which is kept for 7 days. You, or an administrator, can cancel an import that waits or runs; administrators see them on the Overview and the Backups page, and are notified of what was imported.

Importing from a backup does not work on **MySQL** yet.
